About

Experienced surgeon and physician leader with a passion for providing counsel to attorneys and key stakeholders in healthcare.

Dr. James Black is a board-certified, fellowship-trained orthopedic trauma surgeon based in Spartanburg, SC. Since completion of fellowship training, Dr. Black has worked continuously at major trauma centers with his practice 100% dedicated to orthopedic trauma surgery. During that time, he gained extensive experience with the development and administration of orthopedic trauma service lines, with a focus on balancing the complexity of today’s healthcare landscape with ensuring the highest quality patient care. In addition, with the breadth of clinical experience, he can provide attorneys with the necessary expert opinions on complex cases.

Services

Orthopedic trauma expertise where you need it most.

Leadership & Organizational Consulting

Developing, renewing and maintaining orthopedic trauma service lines for hospitals and healthcare systems including clinical protocols, team structure, call management, ACS verifications and operating room efficiency.

Medical Legal Services

Orthopedic trauma surgery expertise available for legal case review, expert opinions and testimony for complex litigation requiring an independent and experienced surgeon.

Short-Term Clinical Contracts

Temporary orthopedic trauma surgery coverage for trauma centers. Hybrid on-site clinical coverage with service line leadership.

Why work with OrthoLumina

Direct physician access. Honest opinions. Clear writing.

Independent by design

Retentions are considered from either side. The goal of a review is a defensible, evidence-based opinion, not an outcome negotiated in advance.

Directly accessible

Case discussion, review, and correspondence are handled directly by Dr. Black. No intake specialist, no associate review.

Written for a lay reader

Reports and testimony are drafted with attorneys, and ultimately juries, in mind: precise medicine, framed in language a non-clinician can follow.
